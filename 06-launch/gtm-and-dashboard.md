# GTM Launch Plan, RouteLogic (B2B)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Feature | One-Click Compliance Checklist |
| Goal | Engagement |
| Launch tier | M, Targeted |

## Goal & Audience
- **Goal:** Engagement, Drivers are currently exiting the workflow application to notify dispatch delivery statuses because the step that should take no more than 3 minutes is taking an average of 14.6 min.  We want to deepen the engagement from the drivers.
- **Target audience:** Drivers

## Launch Tier
- **M, Targeted**, Existing feature is being updated to improve user experience.  The launch requires proactive communication to a targeted audience and a focused GTM plan.  A L multi-channel and full-scale XL campaign are not warranted, but a targeted focused approach is essential to connect with the drivers and increase adoption.

## Channels
1. **Owned: Email: Targeted email to reach drivers introducing the upgraded feature reducing the compliance log step to On-Click reducing the time to complete and need to use communication outside of the application. Second role-specific email to managers or dispatch to notify of the upgrade and encourage adoption.**
2. **Owned: Push Notifications: Reaching drivers already using the application to describe the new One-Click Compliance Checklist with a deep-link to the updated workflow. .**
3. **Owned: In-product demo: Reaching drivers within the app with clear short-form video demonstration of the new feature experience within the existing workflow.**

## Enablement & Assets
Launch emails announcing the upgraded On-Click Compliance Checklist Feature with launch date. 
Push Notification sequence introducing the upgraded feature.
Short-form video demonstrating the new feature experience.

## Ownership, Budget & Timeline
- **Ownership & budget:** PM: Owns the launch plan, coordinates all teams, and monitors post-launch metrics.
Marketing:	Launch email. 
Design:	Owns the demo video.
Engineering: Owns push notification, in-product demo, and performance monitoring on launch day.
Customer Support:	Briefed on One-Click Compliance Checklist before launch. 

No additional budget required.
- **Timeline:** Week 1: Launch Emails with Launch Date 
Week 2: Launch day + push notification + in-product demo
Week 3+: Monitor, collect feedback, iterate

## Success Metrics
- **Metrics:** Checklist completion time: median elapsed time from opening the eligible compliance step to successful submission of the required checklist.
14-day driver adoption: percentage of eligible drivers who successfully complete at least one checklist using the upgraded workflow within 14 days of becoming eligible.
- **Bad signal to watch for:** Daily Active Users but One-Click Compliance Checklist is not being used
- **Likely post-launch decision:** Iterate to adjust the messaging/channel because data is showing mixed signals.
