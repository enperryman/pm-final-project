# Problem Hook & Value Proposition, Module 1

- **Scenario:** RouteLogic Velocity (B2B)

## Strategic crisis
The company will lose the largest enterprise account and potentially other accounts due to the opened gap.

## Moment of misery
They are testing alternate solutions.

## Problem hook
We must solve for the loss of business by addressing the increased administrative burden to better support frontline usefulness.

## Value proposition
For the 95% of users who rely on RouteLogic daily, we will remove extra layers  because we  are at risk of losing enterprise accounts to competitors.

## Cold-read self-review
The business risk is the loss of executive accounts. It is a systemic problem because our clients are actively trying alternate solutions. It is removing obstacles by focusing on the needs of the majority of users.
