## Step 3 · Craft your hypothesis
- **Qualitative evidence (from M2) , quote the specific friction / moment of misery for your persona:** To mark a stop delivered I tap through three screens. In the rain, at a doorstep, with a package in one hand. I've started just texting my dispatcher instead.
- **Quantitative evidence (from M3) , name the metric or data point that confirms the pain; cite the number:** Logging compliance checks takes 11.6 minutes more than the established benchmark.
- **Persona , role, goal, and the friction you confirmed in the reconciliation steps:** Drivers. Completing deliveries quickly and accurately, with minimal time spent interacting with the app.  Logging a completed delivery takes nearly 5x longer than it should and drivers are working around the app by texting dispatch.
- **Problem you are solving , one sentence describing the specific friction this initiative removes:** Navigating multiple screens and actions to log delivery completion.
- **Strategic outcome , what behaviour change do you expect, and how does it map to retention / revenue / churn?:** The driver will no longer exit the delivery workflow application to notify dispatch the delivery status, simplifying the log compliance checks step and increasing usage and decreasing time spent.
- **Primary success metric (initiative signal) , the leading indicator that tells you the gap is closing:** Increase usage of Log Compliance Checks step
- **Guardrail metric (product signal) , the metric that must NOT drop; it protects your existing base:** Manager Satisfaction for Compliance and Reporting must not decrease
- **Decision window , how much time or data before you scale, pivot, or kill? minimum threshold to proceed?:** 30 days to increase usage by 15%
- **Draft your full hypothesis sentence , one to three sentences; quote the metric, name the persona, name the outcome:** Based on user feedback citing a lengthy compliance logging process and the data showing the process taking 11.6 minutes longer than expected, I believe that a simplified log compliance check step for drivers will result in drivers no longer exiting the delivery workflow application to notify dispatch of the delivery status, as measured by a 15% increase in usage of the log compliance check step.  I will protect the manager satisfaction for compliance and reporting and will make a go/no-go decision after 30 days.
